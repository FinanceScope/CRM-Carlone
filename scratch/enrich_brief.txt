Context. Business partners based in Cosne-Cours-sur-Loire (Nièvre, France) phone companies of the Nièvre to offer them original artworks by living artists for their premises (tax deduction of article 238 bis AB of the CGI). This is CRM enrichment: for each company, fill the gaps that make a call better prepared, with a source for every data point. Never mention, in any text you write, an exhibition venue, a restaurant, a bar or the partners' own establishments.

Input: <racine du dépôt>/ext/{INFILE} — an array of company records (legal and trading name, town, address, activity, workforce, group, and what is already known: site_web, email, linkedin, description, actualite). Read them first and do not look again for what is already filled.

Search with WebSearch (load it with ToolSearch; WebFetch and curl are blocked). At most 2 searches per company and at most {BUDGET} for the whole file. The tool returns a summary of the pages, so put the words you need in the query:
1. Company page: "<trading or legal name> <town>" plus "contact" or "mentions légales" (with allowed_domains set to the company's own domain when site_web is known): official website, generic e-mail published by the company (contact@, accueil@, info@, commercial@…), LinkedIn company page and the headcount it shows.
2. Recent news: "<trading or legal name> <town> 2025 2026" or with words like "nouveaux locaux", "agrandissement", "investissement", "inauguration", "anniversaire", "rachat", "nouveau directeur", "recrute": local press (Le Journal du Centre, L'Yonne Républicaine, Le Berry Républicain, Koikispass, France 3 Bourgogne-Franche-Comté), the company's own news, the group's press releases.

Rules:
- Every value needs a source URL that names this company (legal name, trading name or shop sign) in this town or at this address. A namesake elsewhere is not this company.
- E-mail: only a generic address published as is by the company itself (its site, legal notice, own social page); never a personal address (prenom.nom@…), never a guessed one. If "opposition_prospection" is true, return no e-mail.
- News: facts dated 2024 or later, one short French sentence each, with month/year and source; at most 3, most useful first (premises, extension, investment, anniversary, takeover, new manager, award, hiring, local sponsorship or cultural involvement).
- "declencheur": one French sentence for the caller, only if a fact above gives a concrete reason to call now (for example new or renovated premises to decorate, an anniversary, a record year); otherwise null. Do not mention any venue.

Output: write valid JSON (UTF-8, indent 1) to <racine du dépôt>/ext/{OUTFILE}: an array, one object per input record, in input order:
{"siren": "...",
 "site_web": "url" or null (only if not already known or if the known one is wrong),
 "email_generique": {"email": "...", "source": "url"} or null,
 "linkedin": "company page url" or null, "effectif_linkedin": "e.g. 51-200 employés" or null,
 "actualites": [{"date": "MM/AAAA", "fait": "...", "source": "url"}],
 "declencheur": "..." or null,
 "note": one French sentence starting with "Enrichissement (28/10/2026) : " — what was found or searched without result,
 "recherches": number of searches used}
No other keys. Then check the file with:
python3 -I -c "import json,re,sys; d=json.load(open(sys.argv[1])); i=json.load(open(sys.argv[2])); assert [x['siren'] for x in d]==[x['siren'] for x in i]; K={'siren','site_web','email_generique','linkedin','effectif_linkedin','actualites','declencheur','note','recherches'}; [print(x['siren'],'clés') for x in d if set(x)!=K]; [print(x['siren'],'email') for x,y in zip(d,i) if x['email_generique'] and (y.get('opposition_prospection') or not x['email_generique'].get('source') or re.match(r'[a-z]+[.-][a-z]+@', x['email_generique']['email'].lower()))]; [print(x['siren'],'actu') for x in d for a in x['actualites'] if set(a)!={'date','fait','source'} or not re.fullmatch(r'\d\d/20(2[4-9])', a['date'])]; [print(x['siren'],'lieu') for x in d if re.search(r'vieux relais|restaurant|\bbar\b|exposition', json.dumps([x['declencheur'], x['actualites']], ensure_ascii=False), re.I)]; [print(x['siren'],'note') for x in d if not str(x.get('note','')).startswith('Enrichissement')]; print(len(d),'fiches,', sum(1 for x in d if x['email_generique']),'e-mails,', sum(len(x['actualites']) for x in d),'actualités,', sum(1 for x in d if x['declencheur']),'déclencheurs,', sum(x.get('recherches',0) for x in d),'recherches')" <output path> <input path>
