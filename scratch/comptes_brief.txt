Context. A small CRM lists the 100 largest companies of the Nièvre (France) by turnover, from a Pappers ranking photographed on 02/09/2026. For each company the CRM computes a yearly tax-deduction cap (20 000 € or 5 ‰ of turnover HT if higher) and says whether the company is profitable. The reference figures are "ca" (turnover, €) and "resultat" (net result, €) from the ranking, sometimes replaced by "ca_recent" / "res_recent" / "annee_recent" from the national registry. Later research wrote more recent figures in free text (fields "actualite", "description", "note_classement", "corrections", "note_contacts", and the drafted card "fiche_redigee"), e.g. "CA 2025 : 8,19 M€, résultat net 46,8 K€" or "CA de l'exercice clos au 30/09/2025 en recul de 13 % à 10,7 M€ (résultat net 392 K€)". These newer figures are not used yet: your job is to extract them so the cap and the profitable/loss-making flag can be recomputed.

Input: <racine du dépôt>/ext/{INFILE} — an array of records.

For each record, look for a financial year MORE RECENT than the one behind the reference figures, stated in the record's own text. The reference figures usually are the 2024 accounts (2023 for some); when "annee_recent" is set the reference year is that one. If the text gives figures for the same year as the reference, or only older ones, there is nothing to extract.

Output one object per input record, in input order:
{"siren": "...", "rang": N,
 "trouve": true | false,
 "annee": "2025" (the year the financial year ENDS; a year closed on 30/09/2025 is "2025"),
 "cloture": "2025-09-30" or null when the closing date is not stated,
 "ca": turnover in euros as an integer, or null if not stated (convert "10,7 M€" → 10700000, "958 K€" → 958000),
 "resultat": NET result in euros as an integer (negative for a loss), or null if not stated. Never use an operating result ("résultat d'exploitation"), an EBITDA, a gross margin or a group figure as the net result: put null and say so in "remarque".
 "perimetre": "societe" when the figures are those of this legal entity (the SIREN), "groupe" when they are consolidated group figures or figures of another entity,
 "citation": the exact sentence(s) of the record you took the figures from (verbatim, copied character for character),
 "champ": the field name the citation comes from,
 "remarque": one short French sentence when something is uncertain (contradiction between two fields, approximate figure, unit doubt), else ""}
When "trouve" is false, set annee, cloture, ca, resultat, citation, champ to null and remarque to "".

Rules:
- Only figures written in the record. Never compute a figure from a percentage alone, never estimate, never use web search.
- If two fields give different figures for the same year, take the most precise one, and explain in "remarque".
- Group figures ("perimetre": "groupe") are reported but will not be used for the cap: still extract them so we know.
- Write valid JSON (UTF-8) to <racine du dépôt>/ext/{OUTFILE}. Then check it with:
python3 -I <racine du dépôt>/scratch/check_comptes.py <output path> <input path>
and fix every line it prints (a citation must be an exact substring of one text of the input record). Do not modify any other file.
