Context. Two to four business partners based in Cosne-Cours-sur-Loire (Nièvre, France) phone companies of the Nièvre to offer them original artworks, with the tax deduction of article 238 bis AB of the CGI as the argument. The purchase is decided by management, but it is prepared, paid and booked by the finance side. The companies in this file are the 37 priority targets (minus the two whose finance manager is already confirmed). Earlier passes looked for decision-makers in general; this pass looks for ONE thing: the person in charge of FINANCE for each company, by name, so the callers can ask for him or her at the switchboard.

Input: <racine du dépôt>/ext/{INFILE} — an array of company records: name, trading name, town, address, workforce, group, website, registry managers ("dirigeants"), contacts already known ("contact_dirigeant", "contact_finance", "autres_contacts"), earlier research notes ("note_contacts") and, for some, the call card ("qui_appeler"). Read them first: do not look again for what is already known, never return a person the notes say belongs to another company.

Read "contacts_admin" (people already found, often "probable"), "contact_finance" and "note_admin" first. Many "finance" entries found earlier are only the owner tagged "Directeur Financier" by the Manageo directory ("dirigeant fonctionnel selon Manageo"): that is a directory label, not a real finance job. Your job is to find the real in-house finance person (DAF, RAF, chef comptable, comptable, contrôleur de gestion, assistante de gestion / administrative et comptable), or, for a site of a group, the group's finance director who decides for it. If a person already listed is confirmed by a new source dated 2024 or later, return him or her again with fiabilite "confirme" and the new source. If you find positive evidence that a listed person is wrong (left, other company, namesake), write "À retirer : <nom> (<raison et source>)" in the note.

Roles wanted, in this order of usefulness:
1. finance: directeur administratif et financier (DAF), responsable administratif et financier (RAF), directeur financier, secrétaire général, contrôleur de gestion, chef comptable, comptable (in-house).
2. rh: DRH, responsable RH / ressources humaines, chargé(e) RH, responsable du personnel, gestionnaire de paie.
3. administratif: responsable administratif(ve), assistant(e) de direction, office manager, secrétaire de direction, responsable des services généraux.
4. direction_site: for a site that belongs to a group whose head office is elsewhere: the director / manager of the Nièvre site (directeur de site, directeur d'usine, directeur de magasin, directeur d'agence), when he or she is not already in the registry managers.
For a small company (under 20 employees) the answer is often a co-manager or an administrative assistant; record it when a source names that person and role.

Search with WebSearch (load it with ToolSearch; WebFetch and curl are blocked). At most 5 searches per company and at most {BUDGET} for the whole file; spend more on the largest workforces and on group sites. Do not repeat a query described in the notes. Angles, by type of company:
- Site of a group (laboratory network, industrial group, car-dealer group, transport group, group of stores): the group's DAF / directeur financier / CFO / RAF at the head office: LinkedIn, the group's site (équipe, gouvernance, contact), press releases ("nomination", "rejoint le groupe", "directeur administratif et financier"), business press (Les Echos, Le Journal des Entreprises, Traces Écrites, Info Chalon, L'Usine Nouvelle).
- Independent hypermarket, supermarket or DIY store (E.Leclerc, Intermarché, Super U / U Express, Bricomarché, Atac / Auchan): the store's own "responsable administratif", "comptable", "contrôleur de gestion", "assistante de direction" or "responsable administratif et financier": LinkedIn with the banner + town + role word, job offers of the store, the cooperative's store page.
- Family SME: the in-house accountant or administrative assistant (often a family member with a real title, or "assistante de gestion"); job offers ("rattaché(e) au responsable administratif et financier, Mme X"); the company's own site (équipe, contact); local press (Le Journal du Centre, L'Yonne Républicaine, Le Berry Républicain, Kopines); business clubs and networks that list members with their title (CCI Nièvre, UIMM, Medef 58, DFCG Bourgogne, club d'entreprises de la zone).

Use the name and title only (never their e-mails or phones); such a person is "probable" unless a second source dated 2024 or later confirms the current position.
If the notes or a source say that the accounts are kept by an outside accounting firm and that the owner handles finance, say so in the note (firm name if published) and do not invent an in-house person.
Other useful queries:
- LinkedIn, with allowed_domains ["linkedin.com"]: "<company or trading name> <town> financier", "... comptable", "... ressources humaines", "... assistante de direction", "... directeur de site". LinkedIn titles show "Name – Role – Company"; keep only people whose CURRENT position is at this company (not "ex-", not a past experience) and, for a group, at the Nièvre site.

- Press (Le Journal du Centre, Kopines, local news), job offers naming the manager ("rattaché(e) à Mme X, DAF"), group annual reports, public procurement or association pages naming the person with her role.

The search tool returns a short summary, not the page: put the role words in the query itself (for example "<company>" "responsable administratif et financier" or "<company>" DRH Nevers) so that the summary states the title next to the name. A LinkedIn result that shows a person at this company without any title is not enough. If only a first name is visible (masked surname) but the role is explicit, record "Prénom (nom non affiché)".
Use mode "extended" when a standard search returns nothing useful.

Rules:
- Only professional information published by the company, the person's own professional profile, or the press. Never a private address, a private phone, a family member.
- Never guess or build an e-mail address (no "prenom.nom@..." patterns). Keep an e-mail only if it is published as is, for that person, by the company. Keep a direct phone only if the company publishes it as that person's professional line. Otherwise null.
- If "opposition_prospection" is true: names and roles are allowed (direct contact is allowed), but tel_direct and email must be null.
- Each person must be tied to THIS company (legal or trading name) and, for groups, to the Nièvre site or to the head office that decides for it (say which).
- "fiabilite": "confirme" = the company's own site, or a professional profile/press article dated 2024 or later that names the person with this company and role; "probable" = a single older or indirect source, or a role inferred from a title that is not explicit.
