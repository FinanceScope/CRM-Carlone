Goal: find professional contact details for companies headquartered in the Nièvre (58, France), for a B2B calling list. The calls will be about a financial/tax topic, so the key person is whoever decides on finances.

Input: <racine du dépôt>/ext/{INFILE} — a JSON array. For each company, find:

Sources: the company website (contact page, mentions légales), search-result snippets (PagesJaunes, societe.com, annuaire-entreprises, 118712, Kompass, Cylex…), the brand's store page for franchise stores. Record the source URL. If "deja.telephone" exists, keep it unless you find it is wrong.
2. email_generique — a generic company email actually published (contact@, accueil@, compta@, info@…), with its source.
3. site_web — if not already known.
4. decideur_financier — the person who decides on financial matters:
   • the directeur administratif et financier (DAF), responsable administratif et financier (RAF), directeur financier, responsable comptable or contrôleur de gestion, when the company is large enough to have one (usually 50+ employees, or part of a group — for a group, prefer the finance person for this site/company);
   • otherwise the dirigeant (gérant / président / directeur général) — say so with type "dirigeant".
   Give name + exact job title + source. Good sources: the company website (équipe, organigramme, actualités), local press, Kompass company pages (they list executives by function), LinkedIn as it appears in WebSearch results (search e.g. "directeur administratif et financier" "COMPANY NAME" or "RAF" "COMPANY NAME" Nevers — use the result title/snippet only, do NOT fetch linkedin.com pages; you may record the public LinkedIn profile URL shown in the result), the registry (deja.dirigeants) for dirigeants.

When no finance person exists, decideur_financier repeats this person with type "dirigeant".
6. adresse — only when the input "adresse" has no street (just a postcode and town): the street address of the registered office or the main site in the Nièvre, with its source. Otherwise null.
7. autres_contacts — at most 2 other useful professional contacts if found on the way (e.g. directeur de site, RH/CSE), same rules.
